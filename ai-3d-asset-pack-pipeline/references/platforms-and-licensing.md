# Platforms and licensing checklist

Use this as a decision aid, not as legal advice. Re-check the live terms and technical requirements before publishing.

## Platform fit

- **Fab**: investigate first for downloadable digital asset packs, especially when offering engine-compatible files and clear technical metadata.
- **Sketchfab**: use for interactive previews, portfolio evidence, and buyer inspection. A marketplace application may require proof of authorship and a portfolio with meaningful original value.
- **Owned product page**: use for the canonical demo, changelog, documentation, email capture, and direct licensing options.

## Provenance record

For each asset, record:

- reference image path and origin;
- generation prompt and model/tool used;
- generated concept identifier;
- reconstruction tool and version;
- any human edits;
- third-party textures, fonts, code, or sounds;
- applicable licenses and attribution text;
- date and reviewer of commercial approval.

## Listing metadata

Prepare:

- target runtime and supported versions;
- included file formats;
- installation steps;
- usage example;
- approximate complexity or polycount where relevant;
- license scope and restrictions;
- screenshots, an interactive preview, and a short demo video;
- known limitations, especially inferred hidden geometry.

## Risk checks

Do not release until you have checked that:

- no recognizable third-party character, logo, or protected brand is being marketed as original pack identity;
- every bundled dependency permits redistribution under the chosen license;
- the marketplace accepts the file formats and category;
- commercial usage claims match the actual source materials and terms;
- the product page does not call procedural source “game-ready mesh” unless a real import test supports that claim.
